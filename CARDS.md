# Chaos Cards

Each human receives **three randomly drawn Chaos Cards** before a match.

Only the Dealer may play a card during a round.

Maximum one card per round.

The card must be announced **before** the affected AI generates its response.

No Chaos Cards during the Final Boss.

## Shrink Ray

Opponent's next response is limited to **5 words**.

Cannot be used:

- in Five-Word War,
- against the 10-word closing statement in Ridiculous Lawyer.

## Corporate

Opponent must answer like an internal corporate announcement.

## Shakespeare

Opponent must answer in exaggerated Shakespearean language.

## Biblical

Opponent must sound like an obscure prophet condemning the subject.

## Nature Documentary

Opponent must narrate the situation like a solemn wildlife documentary.

## Legal Department

Opponent must include an absurd legal disclaimer.

## No Letter E

Opponent's response may not contain the letter **E**.

## Absolutely Normal

Opponent must treat the absurd premise as entirely routine.

## Minor Inconvenience

Opponent must include the exact sentence:

> This is mildly inconvenient.

This card is intentionally disappointing.
